What we can help with

The right coverage depends on how the content will be used. A product shoot needs to show shape, material and detail. A corporate project may need a consistent set of images for a profile or website. An event needs a plan for key people and moments. Defining those uses early helps the shoot and editing work toward the same outcome.

Corporate photography and video

Visual content for company profiles, websites and business communications.

Product shoots

Photography and video planned around the product, its important details and the intended presentation.

Event coverage

Corporate and private event coverage with key moments, shot priorities and access requirements agreed in advance.

Live content and editing

Live coverage needs and post-production outputs, including the formats and versions required for your channels.

From the brief to delivery

  1. Plan the shoot

    Share the date, location, audience, shot list and intended uses of the images or film.

  2. Coordinate coverage

    Agree the schedule, access, approvals and key moments that must be covered.

  3. Edit for the destination

    Confirm the selection, editing direction, lengths, dimensions and delivery formats in the brief.

Before we start

Do you cover corporate and private events?

Both are included in Awwal's event service offering. Share the date, venue and coverage requirements to discuss availability and scope.

Can photography support a company profile or website project?

Yes. A coordinated brief can connect the shoot to the profile, website or campaign so the content fits its intended use.

What information is needed for a shoot enquiry?

Send the project type, date, location, duration, important subjects and expected outputs. Venue permissions, participant approvals and access requirements should also be identified.

Tell us what you need.

Share your project, audience and timeframe. We’ll discuss the scope and the next steps.

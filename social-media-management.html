Plan your project

Content planning and design

Build a content direction around your services, audience and campaign calendar. Agree the number and type of posts, approval process and publishing responsibilities.

Channel management

Discuss the accounts you want managed, posting schedule and community care requirements. Platform coverage and response responsibilities depend on the selected scope.

Advertising and measurement

Connect content with Google or Meta advertising when needed. Ask which reporting and campaign management are included, and confirm advertising spend separately.

Social media management with Awwal

Before you start

Where can I see your social media plans?

Our digital marketing and campaigns page lists social media plans, pricing and service terms. Review the current plan details there and contact us to confirm a suitable scope.

Can we discuss Arabic and English content?

Yes. State your audience and required languages in the brief so content language, writing and approval responsibilities can be agreed.

Are ads and photography included in account management?

Do not assume they are included. Awwal offers advertising, photography and video services; their inclusion depends on the plan or proposal you choose.

Send your brief to Awwal

Include your city, required service, project goals and deadline. We will discuss the scope before work starts.

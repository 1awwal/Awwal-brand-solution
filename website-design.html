What we can help with

A website should help visitors understand your offer and take the next useful step. We organise the pages around the information people need, then shape the visual design around your identity. For a service business, that may mean clear service pages and enquiries. For an online store, it may include product information, navigation and the purchase journey.

Business websites

Service information, company content and enquiry routes arranged into a coherent website.

E-commerce websites

Store structure, product presentation and the content needed to support an online buying journey.

Responsive design

Layouts that adapt to mobile, tablet and desktop, with readable content and accessible controls.

Hosting and ongoing management

Hosting, updates, maintenance responsibilities and ongoing support can be discussed as part of the project scope.

From the brief to delivery

  1. Plan the content

    Agree the audience, pages, required functionality and materials that need to be prepared.

  2. Design the experience

    Develop the page structure and visual direction, then review the main browsing and enquiry flows.

  3. Prepare the launch

    Review links, forms, device layouts and basic search settings before agreeing the launch and ongoing responsibilities.

Before we start

Can you build a business site and an online store?

Both business and e-commerce websites are part of Awwal's services. The platform, catalogue, payment and integration requirements need to be discussed before a scope is agreed.

Will the site work on mobile?

Responsive layouts are part of the website design brief. Content, navigation and the main actions should be reviewed across common screen sizes.

Can you manage an existing website?

Share the current site and the updates or problems you need help with. Hosting access, platform limitations and maintenance responsibilities can then be assessed.

How this course works

  1. Start at your level. Beginner, Intermediate and Professional each have their own curriculum. New to the subject? Begin at level one.
  2. Watch, then read the notes. Every session has one trusted YouTube tutorial and short session notes with the points to remember.
  3. Do the practice task. Skills come from doing. Every session ends with something to make.
  4. Earn a certificate per level. Mark sessions complete — progress is saved in this browser — and unlock a certificate at each level.

What you need

  • A business to practise on: your own, a friend's, or an invented one.
  • Free accounts: a Meta Business account, Google Ads and Google Analytics. You do not need to spend money to learn the tools.
  • A spreadsheet: Google Sheets or Excel for planning and tracking.
  • Time: about 5 hours of video across all three levels, plus practice.

LEVEL 01 · BEGINNER

Marketing foundations

Understand how customers find, choose and buy — and how every channel fits into that journey.

Session 1.1

Digital marketing in one lesson

A clear overview of the main channels and how they work together.

Video referenceDigital Marketing 101 (A Beginner’s Guide To Marketing In 2026)Adam Erhart · 19:25 · YouTube ↗

Session notes
  • Marketing is getting the right message to the right person at the right time.
  • Owned (website, email), earned (reviews, shares) and paid (ads) media support each other.
  • Choose one or two channels and do them well before adding more.
Practice

List every way a customer can currently find your business and mark each as owned, earned or paid.

Session 1.2

The marketing funnel

The stages a customer moves through from first hearing about you to buying.

Video referenceThe Marketing Funnel Explained: What Is It & How To Write OneAlex Cattoni · 11:01 · YouTube ↗

Session notes
  • Awareness, consideration, conversion, loyalty.
  • Each stage needs different content and a different call to action.
  • Most businesses lose customers in the middle, not at the top.
Practice

Draw your funnel and write one piece of content for each stage.

Session 1.3

Finding your target audience

How to define exactly who you are speaking to.

Video referenceHow To Find Your Target Audience | Target Market ResearchAdam Erhart · 13:11 · YouTube ↗

Session notes
  • If you speak to everyone, nobody feels spoken to.
  • Describe their problem, their goal and where they spend time online.
  • Talk to five real customers — it beats any assumption.
Practice

Write a one-page customer persona with a name, a problem, a goal and three objections.

Session 1.4

Social media marketing

How to build a simple, sustainable social media plan.

Video referenceSocial Media Marketing for Beginners: Step-by-Step PlanMaggie Stara · 23:00 · YouTube ↗

Session notes
  • Pick the platform where your customers already are.
  • Use three or four content pillars so you never start from a blank page.
  • Consistency beats frequency: post at a pace you can keep.
Practice

Choose your content pillars and plan two weeks of posts in a calendar.

Session 1.5

Content marketing

How useful content attracts customers without paying for every click.

Video referenceContent Marketing For Beginners: Complete GuideAhrefs · 16:09 · YouTube ↗

Session notes
  • Good content answers a question your customer is already asking.
  • One strong piece can be reused as posts, reels, emails and stories.
  • Content earns trust slowly; give it three to six months.
Practice

Write down 20 questions customers ask you and turn the best one into a post or short video.

Session 2.1

SEO essentials

How search engines decide what to show and how to earn a place there.

Video referenceLearn 80% of SEO in 14 MinutesAhrefs · 14:33 · YouTube ↗

Session notes
  • SEO has three parts: relevant content, a technically healthy site and trusted links.
  • Target what people actually search for (keywords), not what you call it.
  • One page should answer one main search intent.
Practice

Find ten keywords for your business and match each to a page on your site.

Session 2.2

Meta ads (Facebook and Instagram)

How to set up and run a campaign in Meta Ads Manager.

Video referenceHow to Run Meta Ads For Beginners (Meta Ads Tutorial 2026)Hostinger Academy · 24:31 · YouTube ↗

Session notes
  • Structure: campaign (goal), ad set (audience and budget), ad (creative).
  • Choose the objective that matches the result you want.
  • The creative — image, video and first line — drives most of the result.
Practice

Build a complete campaign in Ads Manager and stop before publishing. Screenshot each level.

You can learn the whole tool without spending: build campaigns and leave them as drafts.

Session 2.3

Google Ads

How search campaigns work and how to set one up properly.

Video referenceGoogle Ads Beginners Tutorial for 2026 (Step By Step)Grow My Ads · 42:08 · YouTube ↗

Session notes
  • Search ads reach people who are already looking for what you sell.
  • Keywords trigger ads; negative keywords block wasted clicks.
  • Send each ad to a page that matches the search exactly.
Practice

Plan one search campaign on paper: three ad groups, keywords, negatives and two ads each.

Session 2.4

Email marketing

How to build a list and send emails people want to open.

Video referenceEmail Marketing Tutorial for Beginners (2026)Metics Media · 17:20 · YouTube ↗

Session notes
  • Your email list is an audience you own — no algorithm in between.
  • Only email people who gave permission.
  • The subject line earns the open; one clear call to action earns the click.
Practice

Write a three-email welcome sequence for new subscribers.

Session 2.5

Google Analytics 4

How to read GA4 to see where visitors come from and what they do.

Video reference(2026) Google Analytics 4 Tutorial for Beginners: How to Use GA4 & Important Data to Look atMariah Magazine · 18:45 · YouTube ↗

Session notes
  • GA4 measures events — everything a visitor does is an event.
  • The acquisition reports show which channels bring visitors.
  • Mark important actions, such as enquiries and purchases, as key events.
Practice

Open GA4 (your own or Google's demo account) and find the top three traffic sources and top five pages.

Session 3.1

Building a marketing plan

How to write a practical marketing plan with goals, channels, budget and timeline.

Video referenceHow to Create a Marketing Plan | Step-by-Step GuideVisme · 11:21 · YouTube ↗

Session notes
  • Start from business goals, then set marketing goals that support them.
  • Make goals specific and measurable, with a date.
  • A plan includes what you will not do.
Practice

Write a one-page 90-day marketing plan: goal, audience, channels, budget and weekly actions.

Session 3.2

Copywriting that sells

How to write clear, persuasive copy for ads, pages and posts.

Video referencePractical Copywriting Course for Beginners [FREE COURSE]Mike Nardi · 37:30 · YouTube ↗

Session notes
  • Write about the customer's problem before your product.
  • Benefits beat features: say what it does for them.
  • Short words, short sentences, one idea at a time.
Practice

Rewrite one of your ads or your homepage headline three different ways.

Session 3.3

Landing pages and conversion

What makes a page turn visitors into enquiries or sales.

Video referenceThe Anatomy Of A High Converting Landing Page | Conversion Rate Optimization TipsNeil Patel · 6:35 · YouTube ↗

Session notes
  • One page, one goal, one call to action.
  • The headline must match the ad or search that brought the visitor.
  • Add proof — reviews, numbers, logos — near the call to action.
Practice

Review one of your landing pages against a ten-point checklist and list five improvements.

Session 3.4

Testing ad creative

A structured way to test ads so you learn what works.

Video referenceHow to Test Facebook Ads Creatives at Every Budget (2026)Dara Denney · 17:06 · YouTube ↗

Session notes
  • Test one thing at a time: hook, visual or offer.
  • Give each test enough budget and time before judging.
  • Keep the winners, replace the losers, and repeat.
Practice

Design a test plan with three different hooks for the same offer.

Session 3.5

Metrics that matter

The numbers that show whether marketing is making money.

Video referenceDigital Marketing Metrics & KPIs: The Only Ones You Need to TrackElevate Digital · 15:41 · YouTube ↗

Session notes
  • CTR and CPC tell you about the ad; conversion rate tells you about the page.
  • CAC is what it costs to win a customer; LTV is what that customer is worth.
  • ROAS = revenue from ads ÷ ad spend.
Practice

Build a simple monthly dashboard in a spreadsheet with five numbers you will track.

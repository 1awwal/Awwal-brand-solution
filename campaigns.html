Included scope, exclusions & delivery

All tiers include a monthly calendar, scheduling, basic resizing and Arabic and English captions up to 100 words per language per asset. Quantities are shared across platforms, not repeated per platform. A carousel up to 5 slides counts as 2 posts. Reels are up to 30 seconds with basic cuts, colour correction, captions and licensed library audio. Basic and Starter allow up to 10 minutes of supplied footage per reel. Included sessions use one creator and a compact kit at one Dammam, Khobar or Dhahran location. Stories, animation, alternate concepts, full crews, talent, studios, raw footage, a separate photo gallery and travel outside those cities are extra. Included campaigns allow up to 2 ad sets and 4 active ads per campaign, weekly optimisation and basic native reporting; creatives use the content allowance. Ad spend and new tracking installations are separate. Monthly batches include 2 consolidated revision rounds. Minimum initial term: 3 calendar months.

Before we start

Can social media support be arranged monthly?

Monthly social media is one of Awwal's service packages. The channels, volume of content, approvals and responsibilities are agreed in the brief.

Is advertising spend included in a project quote?

The proposal should distinguish creative and management work from the budget paid to advertising platforms. Confirm both before a campaign begins.

Can you guarantee sales or a first-place Google ranking?

No. Results depend on the market, competition, offer, budget and platform systems. We can define the work and measurement plan, but cannot promise a particular ranking or business result.

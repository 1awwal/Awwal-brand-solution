Module 1Capture intelligible speech0/2 sessions

Mic position and room sound

Session notes

Move the speaker close enough for direct speech to dominate room reflections. Angle the mic slightly off-axis to reduce plosives. Monitor speech and record a short room-tone sample before anyone moves.

Practice task Record the same line near and far from the mic and compare the room reflections in headphones.

Isolated recording and sync

Session notes

If possible, record each speaker on a separate track. Name tracks and clap once at the start to create an identifiable transient. Keep a backup and listen for drift near the end.

Practice task Record a two-speaker test, align the clap and compare synchronization at the beginning and end.

Module 2Edit dialogue and check the mix0/2 sessions

Assembly and natural pacing

Session notes

Remove false starts and repeated lines, keeping enough breath and conversational pauses to preserve meaning. Add short fades to prevent clicks and match room sound between edits.

Practice task Cut a two-minute conversation to 90 seconds while preserving the speaker's argument and natural rhythm.

Cleanup, dynamics and monitoring

Session notes

Use light noise reduction only where needed and compare with the original at matched loudness. Adjust uneven phrases with gain before shaping peaks. Listen on headphones and small speakers before export.

Practice task Make before-and-after versions with matched playback levels and note two audible improvements.

Module 3Package an episode and teaser0/2 sessions

Masters, metadata and export QC

Session notes

Keep a lossless master and export a distribution copy to the destination’s specifications. Write a title, summary and speaker credits. Play the beginning, edits and end of the exported file and check loudness and peaks against the delivery spec.

Practice task Export a WAV master and a delivery copy and create a QC log with the settings and listening checks.

Teaser and handover

Session notes

Find one complete, understandable moment that works without the whole episode. Add accurate captions and context, then organize the master, assets, credits and approved teaser.

Capstone Deliver a three-minute episode, a 20–30-second teaser with reviewed captions, and an organized handover folder.

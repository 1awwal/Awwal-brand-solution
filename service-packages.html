Planning, approvals & booking

Project scope and client inputs

Provide your business and approval contact, selected services, quantities, platforms, languages, locations, asset formats and target date. The quote records deliverables, exclusions, revisions, client materials, third-party costs, milestones and required access. Service quotations are valid for 15 calendar days; print quotations for 7. Work starts after the agreed payment, brief, access and assets are received.

Payment and recurring services

Projects require 50% advance and 50% after final approval before unrestricted final files, launch or handover. Monthly services are payable before each calendar month; approved external production costs are payable before supplier orders. Social media, advertising and SEO have a 3-calendar-month minimum initial term, with monthly renewal afterward unless at least 30 days’ written notice is given. The accepted quote records any prorated first month, applicable taxes and changes.

Feedback, changes and ownership

Client feedback is due within 3 business days. A revision round is one consolidated written feedback list against the brief; 2 rounds apply unless a package states otherwise. Extra concepts, languages, changed briefs or revisions require an estimate and written approval. Silence is not approval or permission to publish. Final approved original designs and agreed editable files transfer after full payment; unselected concepts, internal templates, video project files and working files remain excluded unless agreed. Third-party licences continue to apply. Client accounts remain client-owned.

Support, rescheduling and outcomes

Social support hours are Sunday–Thursday, 9 am–6 pm Saudi time, excluding public holidays. Routine social requests receive a response within one business day; Pro within 4 business hours. Community work uses approved routine answers and escalates sales and complaints; order handling and continuous customer service are excluded. One shoot reschedule is allowed with at least 3 business days’ notice, subject to availability. Cancellation is reconciled against completed work and approved non-cancellable costs. Sales, leads, rankings and platform approvals are not guaranteed. All changes and acceptance are recorded in writing.

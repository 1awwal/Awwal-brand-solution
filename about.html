ABOUT / AWWAL BRANDS

ABOUT
AWWAL BRANDS.

Awwal — أول — means “first”. We help new and growing businesses in Saudi Arabia’s Eastern Province launch with a clear brand and grow with consistent, connected marketing.

Who we are

Awwal Brands is a branding, design and digital marketing studio based in Dammam, serving businesses across Dammam, Al Khobar, Jubail and the wider Eastern Province. We work with founders, small businesses and established companies that know what they want to become but need help choosing a name, getting the look right and explaining their offer clearly.

Many businesses are already posting, printing and building a website, yet nothing feels connected. Awwal brings your message, design and digital presence together, so you have one clear foundation to launch with confidence and grow from.

What we do

  • Brand identity & graphic design — logos, brand guidelines, company profiles, packaging and print.
  • Digital marketing & advertising — social media management, Google Ads and Meta ads campaigns.
  • Website design & management — fast, bilingual websites with ongoing updates and SEO basics.
  • Photography & video production — product and brand photography, reels and event coverage.

See every service and package in services & packages.

How we work

Every project starts with a short conversation about your business, your customers and your goals. We then agree a clear plan, scope and timeline, design and review together, and hand over finished files you own. For ongoing marketing, one connected team keeps your website, social media and advertising consistent, so you can focus on running your business.

The founder

Awwal Brands was founded by Famin Favas, a creative and operations leader working across brand thinking, media production and digital experiences. Meet the founder.

PRIVACY / AWWAL BRANDS

YOUR DETAILS,
HANDLED WITH CARE.

This policy explains what information awwalbrands.com collects, why, where it is stored and the choices you have. It is written in plain language.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry through the form on this website, we collect the details you choose to give us: your name, company, email address, phone number, the service you are interested in, your budget range and your project details. You confirm that you agree to this before the form is sent. If you contact us on WhatsApp or by email, we receive the details included in your message.

To stop spam, the website briefly records a one-way scrambled (hashed) value based on your connection and the hour you submitted. It is used only to limit repeated submissions and cannot be turned back into your IP address, which we never store.

This website does not use advertising trackers or third-party analytics cookies.

How we use it

We use your details only to reply to your enquiry, prepare quotes and proposals, and deliver the work you ask for. We may contact you by email, phone or WhatsApp about your enquiry. We do not sell your information or use it for unrelated marketing.

Where it is stored

Enquiries are stored securely using Cloudflare, which hosts this website. When an enquiry arrives, a notification with its details is sent to our team on WhatsApp (Meta). We may also manage enquiries and client work in our internal tools. Each provider protects data under its own security and privacy terms.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to respond and, if we work together, for the length of the project and any period required for accounting or legal purposes. You can ask us to delete your details at any time.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or ask us to stop contacting you. Email hello@awwalbrands.com or message us on WhatsApp +966 53 921 1696 and we will respond promptly.

Changes

If we change how we handle your information, we will update this page.

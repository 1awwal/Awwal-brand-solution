How this course works

  1. Choose your track. Learn Final Cut Pro, DaVinci Resolve or both. Craft sessions apply to every track.
  2. Start at your level. Beginner, Intermediate and Professional each have their own curriculum. New to the subject? Begin at level one.
  3. Watch, then read the notes. Every session has one trusted YouTube tutorial and short session notes with the points to remember.
  4. Do the practice task. Skills come from doing. Every session ends with something to make.
  5. Earn a certificate per level. Mark sessions complete — progress is saved in this browser — and unlock a certificate at each level.

What you need

  • Final Cut Pro track: a Mac with Final Cut Pro. Apple offers a free trial; some newer tools need Final Cut Pro 11 or later.
  • DaVinci Resolve track: a Mac or Windows PC with DaVinci Resolve. The free version covers almost every lesson; a few AI tools need Resolve Studio and are labelled.
  • Footage: your phone is enough. Shoot your own clips or use free stock footage for the practice tasks.
  • Time: about 14 hours of video across all three levels, plus practice.

Session 1.4Craft · any app

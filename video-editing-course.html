How this course works

  1. Choose your track. Learn Final Cut Pro, DaVinci Resolve, or both. Craft lessons apply to every track.
  2. Watch the video reference. Each lesson is built around one trusted tutorial from an established YouTube educator.
  3. Do the practice task. Skills come from editing, not watching. Every lesson ends with something to make.
  4. Mark it complete. Your progress is saved in this browser. Finish your track and the capstone to unlock your certificate.

What you need

  • Final Cut Pro track: a Mac with Final Cut Pro. Apple offers a free trial; some newer tools need Final Cut Pro 11 or later.
  • DaVinci Resolve track: a Mac or Windows PC with DaVinci Resolve. The free version covers almost every lesson; a few AI tools need Resolve Studio and are labelled.
  • Footage: your phone is enough. Shoot your own clips or use free stock footage for the practice tasks.
  • Time: about 8 hours of video for the Final Cut Pro track, 9 for DaVinci Resolve, or 14 for both — plus practice.

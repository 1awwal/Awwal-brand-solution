Branding and digital marketing in Dammam and the Eastern Province

Awwal Brands is based in Dammam and works with businesses across the Eastern Province, including Al Khobar, Dhahran, Jubail, Qatif and Al Ahsa. We build brand identities, company profiles, social media content, ad campaigns and websites in English and Arabic. As this is our home region, it is also the simplest place for us to meet in person.

Where in the Eastern Province do we work?

Dammam · الدمام

Our home base. A good fit for new businesses that want a logo, a company profile and first social media content from one team they can meet.

Al Khobar & Dhahran · الخبر والظهران

Retail, food and professional services compete hard for attention here. We connect the brand, the storefront artwork and the social content so they look like one business.

Jubail · الجبيل

Industrial and contracting firms are often judged on paperwork first. We design company profiles and capability documents that are clear and easy to send to a buyer.

Al Ahsa & Qatif · الأحساء والقطيف

Family businesses and local producers often need a tidy identity and packaging. We can refresh a long-standing name without losing what people already recognise.

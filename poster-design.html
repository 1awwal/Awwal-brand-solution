Included scope, exclusions & delivery

Creative batches include 2 consolidated revision rounds, one primary size and one resize per design. Clients supply approved copy and assets. Strategy, publishing, motion, community handling, printing, original copywriting, translation, extra named versions, advanced illustration, rush work and extra sizes are separate. Individual design services include 2 consolidated revision rounds for one selected direction and approved editable layouts after full payment. Typical individual turnaround is 3–7 business days; profiles and manuals take 7–15 business days.

Request a written quotation for your selected service, project brief and target date. Availability and the final scope are confirmed before booking.

What should you know before ordering posters?

Do you design social media posters?

Yes. Request digital artwork with the channel, format and dimensions you need. For regular content planning and account management, see our social media management service.

Is printing included?

Printing is not automatically included in design work. Specify whether you need design files only or want to discuss production requirements.

What should I send for a quote?

Send the message, language, logo, available photos, required sizes, number of designs and intended deadline. The proposal will define the deliverables.

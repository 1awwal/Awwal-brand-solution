Scope & payment terms

Batch scope and client materials

Each price is a one-time project fee for the stated number of unique static creatives and campaign styles. Supply brand assets, guidelines, copy direction and product imagery before work starts. Standard platform resizing is included only where specified in the agreed scope. Extra styles, animation and specialist copywriting are quoted separately. Feedback is expected within 3 business days; late assets or feedback adjust the schedule.

Revision allowances and source files

Growth includes 2 consolidated revision rounds and Premium includes 5. Starter revisions must be agreed in the quote. For Volume, the pricing sheet says “1 revision each” while its detailed terms say “1 revision per asset batch”; the exact allowance must be confirmed in writing before booking. Additional revisions or new creative directions are charged separately. Final approved creatives are delivered after full settlement; editable Photoshop or Illustrator source files are excluded unless separately licensed or agreed.

Payment, production costs and project timing

Projects require 50% advance to book, with the remaining 50% due after final approval and before unrestricted final files are released. Approved third-party production costs are payable in full before supplier orders. Work begins once payment, the brief, access and required assets are received. Overdue payments may pause work after written notice; resumption depends on availability. Delivery dates and VAT treatment are confirmed in the accepted quote.

Do you design social media posters?

Yes. Request digital artwork with the channel, format and dimensions you need. For regular content planning and account management, see our social media management service.

Is printing included?

Printing is not automatically included in design work. Specify whether you need design files only or want to discuss production requirements.

Send the message, language, logo, available photos, required sizes, number of designs and intended deadline. The proposal will define the deliverables.

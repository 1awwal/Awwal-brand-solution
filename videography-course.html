How this course works

  1. Start at your level. Beginner, Intermediate and Professional each have their own curriculum. New to the subject? Begin at level one.
  2. Watch, then read the notes. Every session has one trusted YouTube tutorial, session notes with the points to remember, and a part-by-part guide that opens the video at each timestamp.
  3. Do the practice task. Skills come from doing. Every session ends with something to make.
  4. Earn a certificate per level. Mark sessions complete — progress is saved in this browser — and unlock a certificate at each level.

What you need

  • A camera: a mirrorless camera or a recent smartphone. The beginner level works with a phone.
  • Sound: a simple lavalier or on-camera microphone makes the biggest difference.
  • Support: a tripod. A gimbal and one LED light are useful for later lessons.
  • Time: about 3 hours of video across all three levels, plus practice.

Frame rate and shutter speed

How frame rate and shutter speed create natural-looking motion.

Video referenceThe 180 Degree Rule of Shutter Speed with Video ExamplesPolarPro · 5:59 · YouTube ↗

Session notes
  • Set shutter speed to about double the frame rate: 1/50 at 25 fps.
  • Shoot 50 or 60 fps only when you plan slow motion.
  • Use an ND filter outdoors to keep the shutter slow in bright light.
